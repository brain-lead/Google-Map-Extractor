## 🚀 Core Operational Capabilities

### 1. 🤖 Autonomous AI Co-Pilot & Autopilot (BYOK)
Bring your own API key (or run 100% offline with Ollama) to turn raw map scrapes into qualified sales pipelines:
- **Multi-Provider Support**:
  - **Google Gemini**: Gemini 2.5 Flash, 2.5 Flash-Lite, 2.5 Pro, 3.0 Flash.
  - **Anthropic Claude**: Claude 3.5 Sonnet, 3.5 Haiku, 3 Opus (with prompt caching).
  - **OpenAI**: GPT-4o, GPT-4o-mini, o1, o3-mini (reasoning-aware token budgeting).
  - **Local Ollama**: Run private local LLMs (`llama3`, `mistral`, `qwen2.5`, `deepseek-r1`) completely offline.
- **Autonomous Agent Mission Loop**: Give plain-English instructions (e.g. *"Find 15 independent local restaurants with high reviews and broken/missing websites"*) and watch the AI plan multi-niche searches, inspect websites, read customer reviews, filter chains, and rank top leads.
- **AI Lead Scoring & Technical Audits**: Scores leads by revenue signals, digital pain points, missing SSL, absent mobile viewports, or slow/broken web presence.
- **Automated Cold Pitch Generation**: Generates personalized email subjects, icebreakers, and call openers tailored to each prospect's exact weaknesses.
- **Full Audit Logs & Memory**: Real-time action trace inspector, resumable missions, and rich Markdown/table previews.

### 2. 🗺️ High-Density Polygon Area Scan & Extraction
- **Interactive Polygon Mapping**: Draw custom areas directly on the map and subdivide cities, counties, and districts into dense grids so zero local businesses are skipped.
- **Direct Contact & Social Enrichment**: Crawls target websites in parallel to extract verified email addresses, phone lines, hours, and social profiles (Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, X/Twitter, YouTube).
- **Proxy Pool & Cookie Rotation**: Built-in HTTP/HTTPS/SOCKS proxy rotation and automated session management.

### 3. 📊 Adaptive Dual-Pane Workspace & Multi-Format Exports
- **Interactive Workspace**: Collapsible navigation, expandable split-screen AI Co-Pilot & results table, and saved shortlists.
- **Instant CRM Exports**: Export enriched leads and AI outreach campaigns directly to **CSV**, **Excel (`.xlsx`)**, **JSON**, and CRM-ready formats.

## 📦 Step-by-Step Quick Start Guide

1. **Download Release**: Download **`Google_Map_Extractor_v3.0.zip`** from the **[Releases](../../releases)** section (or clone/download `Google_Map_Extractor.exe` directly).
2. **Extract Files**: Extract the archive to a clean folder on your Windows PC (e.g. `C:\Tools\Google_Map_Extractor`).
3. **Install Certificate (One-Time Setup)**: Right-click **`Trust_Certificate.bat`** and select **Run as administrator** to register Brain Lead's Authenticode certificate into your Windows Trusted Store.
4. **Launch Application**: Double-click **`Google_Map_Extractor.exe`**. Your default browser will open automatically to the local web dashboard.
5. **Connect AI (Optional)**: Open **Settings → AI Engine**, paste your Gemini, Claude, OpenAI, or Ollama endpoint, and test connection to activate the AI Autopilot.
6. **Start Extracting**: Run keyword or polygon area scans, inspect enriched leads, or launch an AI Prospecting Mission.
